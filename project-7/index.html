01 / RESEARCH

UNDERSTANDING THE HUMAN SIGNAL

Project 7 investigates whether measurable patterns within physiological signals can be processed, modeled, and classified through computational methods.

The research builds upon previous experimental experience with EKG signal acquisition, R-R interval analysis, heart-rate measurement, and statistical evaluation.

The objective is to develop and evaluate methods for transforming physiological signals into meaningful computational information.
